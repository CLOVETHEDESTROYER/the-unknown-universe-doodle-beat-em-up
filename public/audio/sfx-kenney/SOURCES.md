# Sci-fi Sounds by Kenney

Source: [Kenney's Sci-fi Sounds 1.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds). The pack is licensed under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). See the included `License.txt` for Kenney's original license and credits.

The game clips are trimmed and faded from the original Ogg files, with high-pass filtering and peak normalization. They use 32 kHz mono PCM WAV.

| Game clip | Kenney source | Source range (seconds) | When it plays |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| `regentPhase-1.wav` | `forceField_002.ogg` | 0–0.95 | Void Regent enters phase two or three |
| `ventWarning-1.wav` | `thrusterFire_000.ogg` | 0.34–1.14 | A nearby Furnace vent or Dread Planet plume starts warning |
