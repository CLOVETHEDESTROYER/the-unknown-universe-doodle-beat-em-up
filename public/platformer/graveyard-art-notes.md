# Graveyard Art

Generated with the built-in Codex image generator, using the existing school rooftop images as visual references. Production PNGs are stored here, not in the generated-image cache.

- `graveyard-moonlit-panorama.png`: original children's graveyard at twilight, hand-drawn colored-pencil texture matching the school panorama; pale turquoise sky, silver/peach clouds, moon, low cemetery skyline with mausoleums, fences, bare trees and warm windows. Sky occupies roughly 70 percent, scenery 30 percent. No characters, platforms, lettering, or card borders. Rendered at 2172 x 724 and used as mirrored parallax panels.
- `graveyard-moss-block.png`: orthographic edge-to-edge platform masonry matching the school block style; pale limestone cap with an emerald moss lip, staggered teal-gray stone courses, sparse carved diamonds and spirals. No scenery, characters, lettering, or perspective. Rendered at 2172 x 724; top 196 pixels supply the cap and the remainder supplies the tiled wall.

Moving lifts reuse the masonry with a subtle mint tint and guide rails. Their collision bodies remain rectangular and separate from the art.
