- The black-and-lime backpack travels from school into the rift, then is dropped in the alley. The folded worksheet survives as the personal keepsake; do not turn it into a different notebook or a magical artifact.
- The meteor's fractured gold-circle mark becomes the physical clue after Level 1. Reuse the actual impact image as its design reference rather than inventing a new emblem in each scene.

- Dark Dragon remains identifiable by the established green head/neck and black horns. Do not treat the distant winged shadow in the transformation image as approval of a new full-body boss design.
- Short first-person X narration for the opening; named speakers when later scenes need dialogue. Keep captions out of generated artwork so scripts and names can be revised independently. The Graveyard ledger's single EARTHIOR heading is an intentional prop detail.

## Gaps to resolve before later batches

- **Initial Ink Blaster:** the game starts X with one, while the origin art correctly shows no weapon before mutation. A pickup/handoff or brief visual explanation during the first chapter can bridge this without giving him the Flame Sword early.
- **Z-God:** the opening establishes his missing sister and the Witchwood ending now supplies her reunion. Her exact power origin is still unspecified; a small stellar arrival does not establish a forced or pre-arrival transformation history. She stays the strongest and invincible; do not manufacture a weakness to make another character necessary.
- **Schoolmate reunions:** Barrett, Nico, Ezra, Kai and C already knew X on Earthior and survived the transportation. Establish their shared history, separated arrival and how they reconnect. Do not invent native origins, other destroyed home planets or forced-service histories. Z is also his sister; exact ages/grades remain unspecified.
- **Local helpers:** Dinio, Doghost and other creature helpers recognize X's kind heart and choose to help because they are kind too. Do not make them schoolmates or dead relatives/pets. C is the explicit playable-schoolmate/ally crossover and remains one person.
- **Optional pickups:** clues and transitions must work without Dinio, Doghost or C being collected. Prepare a physical-clue or recorded-message alternative for indispensable information.
- **Alternate heroes:** the integrated player explicitly presents X GOD'S STORY while preserving the chosen schoolmate in gameplay. No alternate origin or duplicated selected hero is invented. Later ensemble scenes must still avoid duplicating C in his playable/ally roles.
- **World geography:** Witchwood is the planned breach site on the first four chapters' refuge world. The Furnace record and flight black box refer to the same upcoming strike, not two unrelated new meteors.
- **Final resolution:** defeat the responsible Regent and stop the harvesting weapon. Do not reveal a new superior villain, reset Earthior's destruction or resolve the parents' mystery. An eventual rescue on a dark planet is only a future possibility, and that planet is not automatically the current Dread Planet.
