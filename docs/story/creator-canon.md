# Creator-established story facts

These decisions override earlier proposed backstories in the writing drafts.

## Earthior and the schoolmates

- X God was an ordinary child on Earthior before the meteor and his transformation.
- **All playable characters are his schoolmates from Earthior. They were transported to the Unknown Universe and survived.** They are not unrelated native heroes recruited from different home planets.
- Z-God is also his little sister. Attending the same school does not require the same grade or exact age. Her established strongest/invincible role remains.
- Barrett, Nico, Ezra, Teleportation C and Killer Kai share that school connection. Reunions should show recognition, familiarity and shared history.
- Their current designs and abilities remain. The exact origins/timing of each classmate's powers or physical changes are not established by the schoolmate decision. Do not invent a pre-arrival or forced-transformation history as fact.

## Playable characters and helpers

- **Teleportation C is both a playable schoolmate and an ally. It is the same person**, not two separate characters. Do not duplicate him in a scene or give him a native gate-worker childhood.
- The other allies/helpers are creatures and inhabitants encountered in the Unknown Universe. They find X God, recognize his kind heart and choose to help because they are kind too.
- Dinio and Doghost belong to that local-helper group. Do not turn them into Earthior schoolmates, lost family members or dead childhood pets without a later creator decision.
- Helping is a mutual act of kindness and friendship, not a prophecy, ownership or a debt that makes the creatures serve him.

## Parents and the ending

- **Their fate stays unknown during the journey.** Any reveal is deferred until the ending or beyond, and the game may leave it unresolved entirely. No ending reveal has been decided yet.
- A possible eventual direction is that they were teleported to a dark planet and need saving. This is a future possibility, not a confirmed event, location, cliffhanger or promised next chapter.
- Do not automatically equate that possible dark planet with the existing Dread Planet or place his parents in its current ending.
- Do not depict confirmed deaths, graves, ghost parents, a rescue or a reunion. Earthior's destruction remains real without deciding each individual's fate.

## Story production consequence

The first level's ending should be X God's reunion with **his schoolmate Barrett**, who has seen a shipment bearing the meteor's mark. Barrett is no longer a proposed native adult protector testing a stranger. Later introductions of Nico, Ezra, Kai and C must follow the same survivor/reunion principle.

Faction names, the complete power origins and some boss motives remain proposals in the [story bible](story-bible-v1.md). Existing approved imagery can stay; new dialogue and subsequent images must follow the facts above.
