# Level 4 generated parallax panorama

Campaign update: this Dread Planet environment now belongs to **Level 6**, after Level 5's [Starbreak flight](starbreak-flight-v1.md). Original Kilina's [Witchwood chapter](kilina-witch-level.md) occupies Level 4. The remaining notes describe the original background pass.

## Asset

`public/platformer/void-regent-pencil-panorama-generated.png` is a 3:1 generated panorama for the Void Regent's dread planet. Its visual direction follows the first two level panoramas: hand-drawn ink contours and colored-pencil texture, with indigo and plum sky, the coral moon, alien mountain silhouettes, ruined arches, and sparse cyan crystals. The lower horizon remains relatively calm so gameplay silhouettes stay readable.

The level uses the illustration as background art only. Platforms, hazards, and gameplay collision remain separate assets and geometry.

## Parallax

Level 4 now places three copies at 1728 × 576 display size, alternating horizontal flip, with a 0.28 horizontal scroll factor. This matches the slow parallax treatment already used by levels 1 and 2. No gameplay test was run; this milestone is ready for the user's playtest.
