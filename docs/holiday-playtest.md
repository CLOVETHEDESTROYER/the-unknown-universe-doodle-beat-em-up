# Holiday playtest

This is the human feedback pass. Automated browser tests have checked purchases and combat behavior; they cannot tell us whether your son's friends want another run.

## One session: about 20 minutes

Use Easy and X God for the first attempt. Each player should use their own device or browser profile if they want separate progress. Gold and cosmetics currently stay in that browser; there is no account or cross-device sync.

1. Let the player start a fresh campaign and explore without coaching for the first few minutes. Note where they stop, repeat an action, or ask what to do.
2. Watch the first enemy and Lockwing. Did they notice Lockwing immediately? Did the two glowing ports explain the incoming bolts? Could they work out when to attack?
3. Let them finish Level 1. Watch whether they understand the points, gem count, Gold payout, and workshop button.
4. Ask them to choose between a useful supply and saving for a cosmetic. Do they understand why an already-full supply cannot be bought? Can they preview a look and confirm a purchase without help?
5. Continue through the story and Alley Rescue into Level 2. Check that purchased supplies and equipped effects are visible or usable.
6. Close and reopen the game, then check the saved wallet, collection, and campaign. Offer a second run without prompting them to keep playing.

Do not explain every control immediately. A repeated question is useful evidence that the game needs a clearer cue.

## Ask the player

- What was the most fun moment?
- Where did you get stuck or feel something was unfair?
- Did you understand what happened when an attack hit?
- Which shop item would you save for?
- Did any glow, flash, or camera movement make it harder to see an enemy or projectile?
- Would you play another level now? Why or why not?

## What to change first

Fix lost progress, confusing purchases, unreadable attacks, or repeated unfair deaths before adding more items. If several players save for the same cosmetic, add choices in that style. If most players finish with lots of Gold and no goal, add desirable cosmetics before making supplies more expensive. If players cannot afford a needed supply after a clear, revisit prices or reward cues.

For the next balance pass, compare observed earnings with the estimates in `workshop.md`. Those estimates are a starting point, not a substitute for these sessions.
