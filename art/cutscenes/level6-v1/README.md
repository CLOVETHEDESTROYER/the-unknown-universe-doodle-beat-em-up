# After Dread Planet — What Remains

Six-screen finale completed with built-in ImageGen. Continues the approved
ink and colored-pencil story style after the actual Void Regent defeat.

The weapon still needs stopping after the fight. X turns toward protecting
others; Kai and C help evacuate survivors; Z-God effortlessly supports the
collapsing arch; the meteor stays unlaunched. Dawn closes on X's scorched
worksheet and his decision to help more survivors.

Earthior stays lost and the parents' fate stays unknown. No new superior
villain, compulsory optional-helper pickup, new power origin or equipment grant.
