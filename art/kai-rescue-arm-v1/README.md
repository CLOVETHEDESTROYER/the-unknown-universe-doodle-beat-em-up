# Kai's blaster-arm connection

Generated with the built-in image generation tool using the shipped X God blaster and Kai reaction sprites as references. `source.png` is the four-pose arm sheet; `run-source.png` is the eight-pose run sheet. Normalized transparent frames are kept in `frames/` and `run-frames/`; the game uses `public/characters/xgod/kai-arm-v1/arm.png` and `run.png`. The sprite-pipeline normalizer applied one shared scale and bottom-center anchor per strip. The run source has a thin generated divider removed before normalization.

Arm prompt: “Production sprite sheet for an existing 2D side-view game. Reference image 1 is X God's actual shipped sprite: faithfully preserve his lime spiky hair, X eye, white smiley shirt, orange pants, black shoes, thick ink outline and colored-pencil notebook doodle style. Reference image 2 is Killer Kai's armor and blue cyan arm cannon, the inspiration for the gift. Draw X God facing right wearing a NEW compact cyan and cobalt mechanical blaster-arm connection over his forward forearm (not a handheld gun); his hand joins the integrated muzzle, a small luminous cyan ring at the end, with an orange connector so it feels like a gift from Kai. Exactly four separate full-body poses in one evenly spaced horizontal strip: idle ready, slight windup, firing with small cyan muzzle flash, recoil and settle. Same identity, clothes, scale, orientation and ground baseline throughout. Each pose isolated fully within its own equal slot, no overlap, no cropped body parts, substantial transparent gutters. Genuine transparent background. No scenery, labels, text, logos, interface or extra characters.”

Run prompt: “Production sprite animation for a 2D side-view game. Image 1 is the approved X God blaster-arm sprite and image 2 is his existing running sprite. Create one transparent sprite sheet of exactly 8 full-body running poses in two rows of 4 equal slots, left to right, top row then bottom row. He runs to the right, with the same lime spiky hair, X eye, white smiley shirt, orange pants, black shoes and integrated blue-cyan blaster arm of image 1. The arm stays attached to his forearm throughout the cycle. Keep same height, outfit, proportions, clear baseline and ink-and-colored-pencil style in every frame; smooth cyclic leg and body movement. Isolate each pose inside its own slot with transparent gutters; no labels, no background, no scenery, no extra characters.”

The rescue sequence uses shipped fire-sword and Kai reaction poses, with timed energy effects. The gift is persisted in browser storage only at the end of the sequence. In the modern ground campaign it replaces the blaster visuals for grounded idle, firing, and running and uses normal blaster damage, upgrades, and durability; existing jump/crouch poses remain in use. In the rescue scene, C or FIRE demonstrates the arm.

## Equip animation

Built-in image generation created `equip-source.png`, then edited it to remove background artifacts as `equip-clean.png`. It was normalized as eight fixed-size, bottom-anchored transparent frames in `equip-frames/`, packed into `public/characters/xgod/kai-arm-v1/equip.png`. The gift phase plays the poses in order and does not award or allow use of the weapon until the sequence ends. Players with an existing unlock can replay the rescue by interacting with Kai.

Prompt: “Production sprite animation for an existing 2D side-view game on a genuinely transparent background. Reference 1: X God's approved unarmed sprite; reference 2: his approved cyan-blue mechanical blaster arm gift. Exactly 8 distinct full body frames, two rows of four equal isolated slots. X God remains facing right, same bright lime spiky hair, X-shaped eye, white smiley shirt, orange cargo pants, green-black shoes, same ink-and-colored-pencil style and consistent scale, no other character. Animate receiving and equipping the gift: 1 reach forward with empty hands; 2 hold the separate cyan blue arm connection in both hands; 3 lift it up toward his arm; 4 line it up at his forearm; 5 slide his hand into the connection; 6 press it firmly into place; 7 raise the now-attached arm up, cyan ring lights up; 8 ready stance with cannon integrated on forearm. Make the physical connection and hand placement easy to read. Keep full body visible, common foot baseline, ample transparent space between slots; no text, labels, background or overlap.”

Cleanup prompt: “Preserve its exact 2-row 4-column layout, poses, character identity, lime hair, clothing, cyan arm cannon and transparent background. Clean the sprite sheet for a game: remove every red streak, gray halo, cast shadow, green or cyan cloud, stray pixel and unrelated mark outside the character silhouettes. Keep only crisp ink outlined X God and his arm connection. In the seventh frame keep the raised blue arm but remove the energy cloud. No scenery, labels, floor, special effects, or background. Each pose fully isolated in its slot with clean transparent gutters.”
